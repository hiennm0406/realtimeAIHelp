Teaching Philosophy

A three-year-old does not learn a second language by being taught one. They learn it the way they learn everything else — by touching, singing, moving, and hearing the same warm phrases often enough that the meaning arrives on its own. Montessori gave me the discipline behind that instinct: prepare the environment carefully, then step back and let the child choose. So my lessons are short, repetitive in the way children love, and built around materials they can pick up themselves. I never push a child to speak before they are ready — the quiet ones are usually listening hardest — and I treat every child's safety and sense of security as the condition that must be met before any learning is possible.
