Teaching Philosophy

Primary school is where a child decides whether English is something they can do or something they are bad at, and that decision tends to stick. So my first job is to make sure every child in the room gets something right early and often. Montessori taught me the method behind that: prepare the materials carefully, then step back and let the child work — a child who chooses the task will go further than one who is assigned it. In practice my lessons are short on teacher talk and long on student practice, phonics is taught systematically rather than guessed at, and reading is built with texts children can actually manage on their own. Mistakes I treat as evidence of thinking. The quiet ones I never rush — they are usually listening hardest.
